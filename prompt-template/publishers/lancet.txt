   - LANCET (thelancet.com): the whole article renders at once, so the supplements
     are already in the page state when it loads — do not scroll, do not open a
     panel, do not read the body. Find the "Supplementary Material" section (it is
     also an entry in the sidebar section list). Each entry shows a DESCRIPTIVE
     caption — "Supplementary appendix", "Protocol", "Statistical analysis plan" —
     beside a link whose own text is only "PDF (261.92 KB)" or similar. Judge every
     entry by the caption: the link text and the file name (often mmc1.pdf /
     mmc2.pdf, but it can be anything and need not be a PDF) tell you nothing. Take
     whatever name the link serves and never rename it.

     DOWNLOADING IS ONE CLICK. Click the entry's link where it sits in the page and
     the file saves itself. Do not open a new tab, and do not try to read, extract
     or reconstruct the link's URL — find_elements and search_page report only a
     COUNT of matches back to you, never the URLs, so repeating them cannot reveal
     a link. Click each supplement exactly ONCE: a second click on one you already
     downloaded is silently ignored and is not a failure.

     A path in available_file_paths IS the finished download, including one inside
     a ".staging" or temp folder — that is where this pipeline collects files
     before sorting them, and it is moved after you finish. Never wait for it to
     move and never re-click to "make sure".

     NOTHING TO DOWNLOAD: if the page shows no supplementary-material section,
     confirm it once with find_elements, selector a[href*="/attachment/"] — every
     Lancet supplement link matches that. A count of 0 means this article HAS no
     supplementary files: call done straight away saying so. Do not scroll the full
     text, do not open the article PDF, do not detour to ScienceDirect. Conclude
     this only once the article page has really rendered; a Cloudflare / consent /
     paywall screen is not an answer — clear it first.

     A Lancet article should take 2-4 steps. If an action returned the same result
     twice, a third try will not differ: click the link, or finish.
